GUI in Programming

Basic Facts Quiz Application

As my cousin was about to start Year 5 in Primary School, he asked me about what kind of Maths they would learn in the Senior Year Level. Rather than just showing my old Basic Facts papers, I decided that it would likely be much more fun and interactive to have an app to practice!

Although several Math Learning Apps already existed, this one would particularly cater to the criteria in that in NZ Schools and in that specific Year Group. Through Python, I imported the Tkinter library to create the GUI. This was first design in Figma, where I iteratively developed the design to match my cousin's preferences.

The final app has four categories for the fundamental operations learnt during that year level. Whenever my cousin wants a higher or lower difficulty, I can always change it too. Additionally, when a wrong answer was typed, a file would be outputted for practice reviews! The application also has comprehensive iterative documentaiton.

Cloud Storage Solution in Embedded Systems

ESP32 Camera Raspberry Pi Visual Capture Storage

After hearing a relative encounter a small car accident that warranted a benefit for footage along insurance, our family decided on acquiring an all in one dashcam. The idea was that it should be able to capture footage continuously while driving, where the footage recorded would then automatically be stored in an internal local drive, and when back home, the footage is added to cloud based storage. The footage can also be viewed real time in an application interface in mobile devices.

When researching existing product lineups, they were either priced highly, or are embedded in car systems. Additionally, no researched system allowed for seamless local and cloud based storage solutions. By using an ESP32 Camera Module, alongside a Raspberry Pi 4B, I was able to create a secure shell connection between the two, where the camera module records the footage whilst communicating with the Pi to stored locally securely. A web server then exists within an application to view that footage. The Pi then connects to the router through a Static IP for the footage to then automatically be stored in a secure database built through Apache Derby.

Custom Designing in 3D Computer Assisted Design (CAD)

Personal Bike Commission Proposal Design

Having a friend that works in a bike store has many benefits. Most of it pertains to how to maintain, restore, or improve a bike, but another plus is the ability to ask about options. A vision I had in mind was a bike of specific parameters and measurements. From the crankset connector, to the ISO pipe, and the front roller chain sprocket, I needed a way to present my ideas to create my ideal bike.

Apart from researching materials, I decided to compile my vision through an actual medium: Computer Assisted Design in 3D. With my prior experience in 3D design, I was able to create the proposed product I had in mind, compiling 23 individual parts with materials adhering to ISO standards, and inorganic shapes with specified dimensions fit of standard.

In the end, the product was my vision, so the overall costs would have compounded and it was why the design is still currently on standby.
